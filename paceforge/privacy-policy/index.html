PaceForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PaceForge (the “App”), developed by Marcin Książek (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on iOS or Android.

1. Overview

PaceForge is a fitness and indoor-training application. The App does not require registration or a user account. Workout information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your workouts or heart-rate measurements.

2. Information processed by the App

Depending on the features you use, the App may process workout settings, workout history, timestamps, exercise performance information, heart-rate readings, and data exchanged with connected fitness equipment such as treadmills and Bluetooth heart-rate sensors. This information is used to display, record, and manage your workouts and to provide the training features you choose to use.

3. Storage and sharing

Workout and sensor data handled by PaceForge is stored locally on your device. We do not collect this data on our servers, sell it, or share it with advertisers or data brokers. PaceForge does not require a cloud account. Data may be included in device backups managed by your operating-system provider, depending on your device settings; those backups are controlled by that provider, not by PaceForge.

4. Bluetooth and device permissions

PaceForge uses Bluetooth to discover and communicate with compatible fitness devices. Relevant operating-system permissions are requested when needed. Bluetooth information is used for device connectivity and workout functionality, not for advertising or location tracking. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

5. Analytics and advertising

PaceForge does not use in-app advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics services to track your activity.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored workout data remains on your device until you remove it using available App features or delete the App and its local data. Removing the App generally removes its local data, subject to operating-system backups or other device-level retention mechanisms. Because we do not receive your workout data, we cannot access or delete it remotely.

7. Children's privacy

PaceForge is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on our servers.

8. Security

Keeping data on your device limits transmission of workout information to us. Device security, permissions, and backups are managed in part by your operating system. No method of storage is completely risk-free.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if PaceForge's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Marcin Książek at marcin.ksiazek@gmail.com.
